CARDIAC CRITICAL CARE PROTOCOL

The STEMI Golden Hour: "Time Is Muscle"

In an acute myocardial infarction (heart attack), every 30 minutes of delay doubles the risk of permanent heart failure. Follow this emergency protocol immediately upon severe chest pressure radiating to the arm or jaw.

STEP 01
Recognize & Stop
Sit upright. Avoid any exertion, climbing stairs, or driving yourself.
STEP 02
Chew Aspirin
Chew 300mg Soluble Aspirin immediately if no known bleeding disorder.
